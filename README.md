# Heart-Disease-Analysis-
 This dashboard analyzes 9,000 patients to uncover key insights into heart disease prevalence and risk factors.

Cases & Demographics: 2,727 patients diagnosed with heart disease (30.3% rate), average age ~54 years, average BMI ~25.

Lifestyle & Risk Factors:

Smoking status strongly influences disease rate (highest among current smokers).

Exercise-induced angina and family history show clear associations with heart disease.

Age & Gender Trends:

Disease prevalence rises sharply with age, peaking in the 70+ group.

Male patients show higher cholesterol levels compared to females.

Symptoms & Chest Pain Types:

Typical angina patients have the highest disease rate (48%).

Asymptomatic cases still show significant risk (25%).

This dashboard provides an interactive view of age, gender, lifestyle, cholesterol, and chest pain patterns, helping identify critical factors for heart disease prevention and management.
